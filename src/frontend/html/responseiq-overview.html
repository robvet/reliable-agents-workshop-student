01 / The system

What Reliable Agents is

Reliable Agents pairs AI with built-in guardrails. A coordinator manages the conversation; specialists handle the actual work; every answer is checked before it reaches you. The proving ground here is grid operations: tracking assets, managing outages, dispatching crews, and coordinating restoration.

Operational AI, kept on a leash

  • Ask in plain English - about assets, outages, crews, or performance history.
  • A control layer decides what happens next - the AI never acts on its own.
  • Every answer is checked for accuracy before it ever reaches you.

A coordinated specialist team

  • One coordinator manages the whole conversation, start to finish.
  • Four specialists handle assets, outages, crew dispatch, and performance history.
  • One secure, read-only connection to your data - no way to change records by accident.

02 / Supported today

Key use cases

Current capabilities center on grid asset tracking, outage management, crew dispatch, and restoration: understanding what happened, tracing impact, finding operational resources, and answering connected questions.

Outage response

  • Report, confirm, and track outages through to restoration.
  • Summarize impact and current restoration status.
  • Connect outage facts with affected infrastructure.

Asset topology and impact

  • Trace substation, feeder, and transformer relationships.
  • Locate assets on the operational map.
  • Follow affected network paths and connected locations.

Crew dispatch and restoration

  • Review crew availability and operating-region coverage.
  • Assign crews to outages and track restoration progress.
  • Compare active outages with available response capacity.

Reliability and recurring issues

  • Explore historical event patterns and repeat offenders.
  • Ask cause, duration, and performance questions.
  • Combine reliability history with asset and outage context.

Cross-domain investigation

  • Coordinate several specialists for one multi-part question.
  • Carry verified findings from one step into the next.
  • Return one clear answer, with a visible paper trail of how it got there.

03 / Why it's safe to trust

How it works

Every request follows the same guarded path - the AI never acts alone, never touches your data directly, and never leaves you guessing why it did something.

It listens carefully

  • Figures out exactly what you're asking - and only that.
  • Only ever hands your request to the right specialist, never a random guess.
  • Handles both simple questions and ones that need several experts working together.

It never runs wild

  • Takes one careful step at a time - never an open-ended chain of actions.
  • Every step is checked against your business rules before it happens.
  • Automatically stops itself - it can't loop forever or wander off-task.

It can look, but never touch

  • Reads your data to answer questions - accurately, every time.
  • Physically cannot write, change, or delete anything in your systems.
  • The map and dashboards draw from that same trusted, read-only source.

It shows its work

  • You see each step as it happens, not just a final answer out of nowhere.
  • Every answer comes with a clear, human-readable explanation.
  • If something goes wrong, it fails safely and tells you why - it never guesses.
